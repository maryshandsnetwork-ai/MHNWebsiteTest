Mary's Hands Network · Inaugural Benefit Gala · January 8, 2027

The In Her Honor Awards

Five awards, presented on January 8, to the people and organizations whose work has honored the women of our community.

Nominations open October 5 and close November 15

The Five Awards

1

Award

Pinnacle of Health Excellence Award

For a leader whose work represents the highest standard of excellence in women's health, well-being, and dignity. This award recognizes someone who has consistently elevated the quality, accessibility, and compassion of care available to women and families in Louisiana.

Nominate for this award →
2

Award

Empowerment Luminary Award

For a visionary who creates pathways of empowerment and opportunity to uplift women. This award honors someone who has opened doors, built bridges, and worked to ensure that women have the knowledge, resources, and support they need to lead their own lives and births.

Nominate for this award →
3

Award

Distinction of Advocacy Award

For a champion whose advocacy advances justice, equity, and women's rights. This award recognizes someone who has spoken up, shown up, and fought for the policy changes, community protections, and systemic shifts that make a real difference in the lives of women and families.

Nominate for this award →
4

Award

Legacy of Care Award

For an organization or leader whose long-standing service has shaped generations of support for women. This award honors sustained, committed presence, the kind of care that does not come and go but stays, grows roots, and builds something that lasts.

Nominate for this award →
5

Award

Rising Impact Award

For an emerging leader whose early contributions already demonstrate extraordinary promise. This award is for the person who is just getting started and whose work is already changing lives, the kind of leader whose best years are still ahead.

Nominate for this award →

Who Can Be Nominated?

Anyone in the community can nominate someone they believe deserves recognition. You can nominate an individual, a team, or an organization. Self-nominations are welcome.

Nominees should have demonstrated meaningful impact in Louisiana, though their work may extend beyond the state. There is no membership requirement.

  • Individuals, teams, and organizations are all eligible
  • Nominees do not need to be affiliated with Mary's Hands Network
  • Self-nominations are accepted
  • Anyone may submit a nomination, no account or membership required
  • A person or organization may be nominated for more than one award
