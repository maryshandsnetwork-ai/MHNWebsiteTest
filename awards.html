In Her Honor: A Legacy in Lavender Awards Gala  |  January 8, 2027

The In Her Honor Awards

Five awards. Five ways our community shows up for women.
Nominations open October 15.

Get gala tickets How to nominate

In her honor.

She is your mother. Your grandmother. Your aunt, your niece, your cousin. She is the woman at the checkout counter and the girl who just found out. She is every woman who has carried a pregnancy, labored through a birth, or stood beside someone who did; the ones who came before us and the ones who are coming next. The In Her Honor Awards go to the people and organizations who have done something in her honor: who have protected her, empowered her, cared for her, and made this community safer for the next woman to walk through the door.

Every birth deserves a doula.

The Awards

Pinnacle of Health Excellence Award

For a leader whose work represents the highest standard of excellence in women's health, well-being, and dignity.

This award recognizes an individual who has set an extraordinary standard in women's health. Whether through clinical excellence, research, education, or community practice, they have shaped what it looks like to truly center women's well-being and dignity in every aspect of their work.

Criteria:

  • Demonstrated measurable impact on women's health outcomes in Louisiana or beyond
  • Consistently places women's dignity and autonomy at the center of their practice
  • Recognized as a model of excellence by peers and the communities they serve
  • Minimum three years of sustained contribution in their field

Empowerment Luminary Award

For a visionary who creates pathways of empowerment and opportunity to uplift women.

This award honors a person who builds the scaffolding that allows women to rise: programs, training, mentorship, access, and opportunity. Their work opens doors that were closed and creates new ones where none existed.

Criteria:

  • Has created or expanded opportunities that directly empower women in our community
  • Work is rooted in listening to and centering the voices of the women they serve
  • Impact can be traced to real, tangible outcomes for real women
  • Approaches their work with vision, creativity, and relentless follow-through

Distinction of Advocacy Award

For a champion whose advocacy advances justice, equity, and women's rights.

This award goes to the person who fights for women when the systems and structures will not. They speak loudly when it is uncomfortable, stand in rooms where women have historically not been heard, and push for change at every level from the bedside to the legislature.

Criteria:

  • Has advocated publicly and persistently for women's rights, equity, or birth justice
  • Work confronts systemic barriers and challenges structures that harm women
  • Has generated meaningful policy, institutional, or community change
  • Their advocacy is grounded in lived experience, whether their own or those they represent

Legacy of Care Award

For an organization or leader whose long-standing service has shaped generations of support for women.

This award honors the long game. It belongs to an organization or individual who has been showing up for women for years, whose service has become part of the fabric of this community, and whose impact can be measured in generations. The Legacy of Care is the only In Her Honor Award that may be given to an organization.

Criteria:

  • Minimum ten years of continuous service to women in Louisiana
  • Has supported a measurable and sustained number of women and families
  • Recognized by the community as a trusted and consistent presence
  • Models care, dignity, and respect for every woman who comes through their door
  • Open to individuals or organizations

Rising Impact Award

For an emerging leader whose early contributions already demonstrate extraordinary promise.

This award belongs to the person who is just getting started and is already impossible to ignore. They are building something, changing something, and making the kind of impact that most people take decades to achieve. We honor them now because the work cannot wait.

Criteria:

  • Within their first seven years of work in women's health, birth, or advocacy
  • Has already generated measurable impact beyond what is expected at their stage
  • Demonstrates vision, drive, and commitment that points to a long career of service
  • Supported and recognized by peers and mentors in the field

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can nominate?

Anyone. You do not have to be affiliated with Mary's Hands Network. If you know someone doing extraordinary work for women in this community, we want to hear about it.

Can I nominate myself?

Yes. Self-nominations are welcome and reviewed with the same care as any other submission.

Can I nominate an organization?

Yes, for the Legacy of Care Award only. All other awards are given to individuals.

Who chooses the recipients?

[SELECTION COMMITTEE STATEMENT]An independent selection committee reviews all nominations. Committee members are announced before the gala.

Can someone win more than one award?

Yes. If someone meets the criteria for more than one award, submit a separate nomination for each. The selection committee considers each nomination independently.

When will recipients be notified?

Recipients are notified privately before the gala so they can make arrangements to attend. All awards are presented at the In Her Honor Awards Gala on January 8, 2027 at the Renaissance Hotel in Baton Rouge.
