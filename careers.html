We're hiring

Network Doula

Where

Baton Rouge, hybrid

Role

Full-time W-2 staff role

Reports to

Breea Carter, Operations Director

What you'll do

You will support families from intake through postpartum as part of the MHN network. You walk with families from the first intake through the postpartum weeks, alongside the volunteer doulas and staff of Mary's Hands Network. No doula certification is needed to start. We provide a path to training and certification, with hands-on mentorship on the job, and room to grow into childbirth education and lactation support.

Schedule

Core hours are Tuesday to Thursday, 9 to 4, with an alternating on-call rotation.

Caseload

The caseload ramps up from 2 families, to 3, then 4 to 5.

Requirements

  • No doula certification needed. We provide a path to training and certification.
  • One year of customer service or case management experience is preferred, not required.
  • Bilingual English/Spanish is strongly preferred.

How to apply

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Send your resume to info@mhndoula.com.
