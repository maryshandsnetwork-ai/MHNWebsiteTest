Earn & Learn Opportunity

Network Doula

Baton Rouge, Louisiana Full-time · On-call $12.50–$15.00 / hour

No doula certification? No problem. This is a full-time, paid position designed to turn the right person into a trained, certified doula. You’ll earn a paycheck from day one while receiving hands-on training, mentorship, and a pathway to professional certification — all on the job.

General Requirements

  • Minimum 1 year of experience in customer service, client coordination, or case management
  • Reliable transportation and valid driver’s license
  • Availability for on-call coverage, including evenings and weekends
  • Must pass background check and drug screen
  • Bilingual (English/Spanish) strongly preferred

Benefits

  • $12.50–$15.00 / hour
  • Hands-on training & mentorship
  • Growth into childbirth education & lactation support
How to Apply

Submit the application form below AND your resume and cover letter to info@mhndoula.com.
