You're Invited

An evening given in her honor.

Mary's Hands Network is honored to welcome you to our very first In Her Honor Awards Gala: A Legacy in Lavender.

This gala exists to support Mary's Hands Network and the volunteer doula care we provide for the women of our community, so that no woman has to give birth alone. It is also an evening to honor the community members who have helped make real change. Mary's Hands Network has done none of this work alone. We are grateful to so many in this community who have stood up to fight for maternal and infant health in a state that so desperately needs it.

We gather for the births we have walked alongside, for our own births, in honor of our own mothers, and for the mothers expecting now, the mothers still to come, the young mothers and the seasoned mothers, all mothers. Tonight we give in her honor, and to carry forward the work of Mary's Hands Network, so that every mother has the opportunity to be supported.

When we extend Mary's hands further, they can reach more women, more families, and more communities, into the light of service, compassion, and support.

The Evening

An evening of celebration, connection, and impact.

Join us for dinner, an open bar, entertainment, and an awards program honoring the people and partners advancing maternal and infant health in Louisiana.
