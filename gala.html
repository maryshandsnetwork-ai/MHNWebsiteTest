In her honor.

She is your mother. Your grandmother. Your aunt, your niece, your cousin. She is the woman at the checkout counter and the girl who just found out. She is every woman who has carried a pregnancy, labored through a birth, or stood beside someone who did; the ones who came before us and the ones who are coming next. This evening is given in her honor. And the awards we present go to the people and organizations who have done something in her honor: who have protected her, empowered her, cared for her, and made this community safer for the next woman to walk through the door.

Every birth deserves a doula.

You're Invited

An evening given In Her Honor.

Mary's Hands Network is honored to welcome you to our very first In Her Honor Awards Gala: A Legacy in Lavender.

The In Her Honor Awards Gala is an evening dedicated to the mothers at the heart of our work, and to the community leaders, partners, and volunteers advancing maternal and infant health across Louisiana. Together, we celebrate their impact and the families whose lives have been changed.

Proceeds from the evening benefit Mary's Hands Network, sustaining the volunteer doula care we provide to families at no cost so that no mother faces birth alone. Your presence honors the women we serve and helps extend that care to many more.

When we extend Mary's Hands Network further, we can reach more women, more families, and more communities and bring them into the light of service, compassion, and support.
