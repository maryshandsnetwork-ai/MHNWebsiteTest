Why Sponsor

Your support becomes a Legacy in Lavender.

Every mother we serve is met by a doula in a lavender jacket. When you sponsor the gala, you place another lavender jacket beside another mother.

Impact

Create healthier outcomes for mothers, babies, and families. Your investment supports maternal health access, doula training, and critical resources.

Visibility

Align your brand with a respected community cause, reach influential community, healthcare, and business leaders, and receive meaningful recognition before, during, and after the event.

Legacy

Demonstrate leadership in advancing maternal and infant health, and a commitment to healthier communities and stronger futures for families.
