Draft for review

Privacy policy.

This page is a plain-language draft for Mary's Hands Network to review. It is not a finished legal policy, and it is not legal advice. It describes only the tools this website uses today. Once this version of the site is live, that includes Google Analytics 4.

Who we are.

Mary's Hands Network is a Louisiana 501(c)(3) nonprofit. EIN 92-2430838. Our office is at 451 E. Airport Avenue, Ste. A, Baton Rouge, LA 70806. Email info@mhndoula.com or call (225) 892-4906.

What you can send us.

The contact page asks for your name, email, an optional phone number, and a message. The form on the page is written to send that information through Formspree. The form address on the site is still a placeholder, so this draft does not claim that Formspree is receiving messages yet.

Applying for doula support, and applying for the Tau Cohort training, opens a form on Monday.com. Those forms collect what you type there, such as contact details and the answers the form asks for. Monday.com stores that information under its own practices. We use it to respond to your application.

Donations and store purchases leave this site and are completed on PayPal. PayPal receives the payment details you enter there. We do not see your full card number on maryshandsnetwork.org.

What the site loads.

Pages load a Metricool script (tracker.metricool.com). We use Metricool to understand visits. This draft does not describe Metricool's cookies or retention, because those details are not visible in our pages. Read Metricool's own policy if you want that account.

Once this version of the site is live, pages load Google Analytics 4 through the standard Google tag, measurement ID G-V7LV29VQYX. We use it to understand visits, including which pages people open. Google receives that visit information under its own practices. This draft does not describe Google's cookies or how long Google keeps the data, because those details are not set in our pages. This page is still a draft for review.

What we do not do.

We do not sell personal information. We use what you send us to answer you, consider an application, or record a gift.

Questions.

Write to info@mhndoula.com if you want to know what we have from you, or if something on this draft should change before it is published as the policy.
