Qué es una doula

Alguien de tu lado, capacitada para quedarse.

A doula is a non-clinical support person who stays beside you during labor and the days afterward. Doulas don’t replace nurses, midwives, or doctors. They sit alongside the medical team so you have a steady hand, a familiar voice, and answers when you need them.

Lo que hacen Apoyo

  • Continuous emotional supportthrough pregnancy, labor, and the early weeks
  • Physical comfort measurescounter-pressure, hip squeezes, rebozo, birth ball
  • Evidence-based informationso you can make informed choices about your care
  • Stay through transitionsnurses change shifts; your doula does not
  • Help you advocatetranslating your hopes into clear questions
  • Non-pharmacological pain supportbreath, movement, water, music, touch

Lo que no hacen Clínico

  • Diagnose or treatmedical conditions
  • Perform clinical assessmentslike vitals, vaginal exams, or fetal monitoring
  • Prescribe or administerany medications
  • Override your care teamnurses, midwives, and doctors lead medical decisions
  • Give medical advicethat responsibility belongs to your medical team
Por qué llevamos el nombre de María
We doula like the original doula. Continuity, presence, showing up. That is the work.

Named for Mary of the Visitation, who traveled to the hill country to stay beside her cousin Elizabeth for three months. No advice, no intervention. Only presence.
