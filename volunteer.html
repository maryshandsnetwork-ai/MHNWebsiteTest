Volunteer with Mary’s Hands Network.

One mission. Many ways to serve. Whether you are a trained doula, a new student, or someone who simply wants to support families in your community, there is a place for you. Our volunteers make free, equitable, high-quality perinatal support possible throughout Louisiana.

Every role helps. Every hour matters. Every person belongs.

Volunteer pathways

Choose the path that fits you.

No medical or nursing experience is required to help.

Pathway 01

Community Birth Doula

Non-medical informational, emotional, and physical support through pregnancy, labor, and postpartum.

  • Virtual and in-person prenatal visits
  • Labor support at the hospital or home
  • Postpartum check-ins through 8 weeks
  • Continuity of care from one face
Pathway 02

Administrative Support

Help us run a tight ship. Our administrative volunteers are essential to client and volunteer success.

  • Client intake and onboarding
  • Scheduling and communications
  • Scholarship coordination
  • Data entry and outcomes tracking
Pathway 03

Outreach & Events Crew

Represent MHN in the community and help our programs shine.

  • Event setup and cleanup
  • Tabling at community events
  • Support during trainings and retreats
  • Photography and storytelling
Who can volunteer?

We welcome individuals who are:

  • Passionate about maternal and infant health, equity, and community care
  • 18 years or older (some roles open to high school volunteers)
  • Able to commit to training, scheduling, and showing up consistently
  • Doulas, trained or in training, ready to serve free of charge
  • Professionals with skills to share: administrative, media, interpreting, and more
